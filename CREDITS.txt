TTYDYN — Credits
================
All visual content in this build is procedural (particles coloured per material,
programmatically generated pixel GUI). No third-party sprites are bundled.

Recommended free asset sources for future art passes (CC0 / permissive):
- Kenney.nl — Kenney UI Pack / Pixel UI Pack (CC0 1.0) — https://kenney.nl
- OpenGameArt.org — CC0 pixel GUI packs — https://opengameart.org
- itch.io free pixel-art GUI packs (check per-pack licence before shipping)

Code: original, written for this project. Material property values are
engineering approximations for gameplay, not certified ballistic data.
Vehicle cutaways are generic "‑like" layouts, not reproductions of any
classified armour arrangement.
